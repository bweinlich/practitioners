# The mail round: each practitioner looks at their page and improves their own entry here

Benedikt decided on 06.11. that every practitioner improves their own profile through this
repository, after looking at what their page shows. Their pull request is their yes
(`CONTRIBUTING.md`, skill `practitioner-bio`, route Request). This replaces the reply-by-mail route
and the earlier proposal to take the repository private.

## What each mail carries

One mail per person, in their language, drafted and never sent by an agent; a founder checks and
sends it. The existing Gmail drafts „Deine Profilseite auf unserer Website" are replaced by this
version.

- Their page, where the website has one: `https://www.hackersandwizards.dev/team/<slug>/`
- Their folder: `https://github.com/hackersandwizards/practitioners/tree/main/src/content/practitioners/<slug>`
- How: `https://github.com/hackersandwizards/practitioners/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md`
- What is still missing and the links h&w proposes, from the lists below.
